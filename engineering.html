Parametric Optimization of an Anthropomorphic Myoelectric Upper-Limb Mechanism

SolidWorks · Fusion 360 · FEA Stress Analysis · Ansys

This project documents the physical assembly, component architecture, and mechanical engineering layouts for an adjustable upper-limb mechanism. Built entirely within parametric CAD suites, the design features custom linkage geometry to maximize force-transmission efficiency during finger contraction. The physical joints incorporate nested structural channels to safely protect sub-miniature actuator lines while minimizing overall physical bulk. Structural mass reduction was handled through algorithmic load path analysis, focusing material placement strictly along regions experiencing active mechanical stress.

Engineering Note: Resolving point-load concentrations at joint connections required replacing standard inline pin configurations with double-shear bracket profiles. This geometric optimization redistributed structural stress smoothly across the mounting face, preventing premature fatigue failure under heavy cyclic loads.

Computational Fluid Dynamics & Transient Flow Optimization of a Microfluidic Sorting Matrix

Fusion 360 · SimScale · CFD Fluid Dynamics · OpenFOAM

To optimize internal flow mechanics within tight diagnostic microchannels, I designed and evaluated a fluidic separation manifold using computational fluid dynamics simulations. The geometric profile channels laminar flow paths to smoothly guide suspended microparticles through sudden turns without triggering boundary layer separation or localized eddies. The structural boundaries and inner transition radii were tuned iteratively to minimize internal stagnation points, keeping pressure values balanced and stable across the active mixing zones.

Engineering Note: High wall shear stresses inside microfluidic networks can easily damage fragile cargo. Transitioning from sharp interior walls to gentle, mathematically filleted boundaries dropped local shear levels below critical thresholds while keeping throughput rates completely steady.
